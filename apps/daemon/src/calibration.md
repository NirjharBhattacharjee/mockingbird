# Calibration script

`mockingbird calibrate` shows these sentences one at a time and compares what
Whisper heard with them. Only lines starting with `- ` are read out.

Write numbers the way Whisper writes them (`7:45`, `48`), or every reading
counts them as wrong. Keep names out: those are the user's own, on a later
page (#108).

- Thank you for helping me set this up today.
- The weather was warm, so we walked along the river after lunch.
- She sells seashells on the shore every summer.
- Three thin thieves thought about the theory of thermal energy.
- Very few wolves were seen in the valley last winter.
- Please write the results on the whiteboard before the review.
- Red lorry, yellow lorry, rolled slowly along the rural road.
- The train leaves at 7:45 and arrives just after 10:30.
- Our team grew from 12 people to 48 in under two years.
- Could you check whether the cheap chips are in the kitchen cupboard?
- I usually measure the treasure with a ruler, not a guess.
- The judge urged everyone to stay calm and keep the change.
- Did you finish the draft, or should I send it to Maria first?
- We need to schedule a call with the design team next Tuesday afternoon.
- My grandmother grew tomatoes, peppers and fresh basil in her garden.
- The engineer explained the problem clearly, but nobody had a quick fix for it.
- Ask the bank for a new card if the old one stops working.
- Six sticky skeletons stood silently in the street.
- Whether it rains or not, the festival will go ahead on Saturday.
- The library opens early in the morning and closes late at night.
- When I was younger, I wanted to be a pilot, a painter or a chef.
- If the build fails again, roll back the change and open an issue.
- Honestly, I think the second option is better, although it costs a little more.
- Before you leave, please turn off the lights and lock the back door.
- It was a long journey, and by the time we reached the village, everyone was tired, hungry and ready to sleep.
